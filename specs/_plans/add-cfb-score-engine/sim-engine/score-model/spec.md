# Feature: Score model

Every simulation, every weekly prediction and every win probability starts from one question: by how much should the home team win this game, and how sure are we? The score model answers it for every game still to be played at any point of a season, from what was known at that point only. Each team's strength is its SP+ rating from the previous season combined with its current Elo. Margins are normal around the predicted margin, and the uncertainty grows with the weeks until the game.

## Background

- **Inputs:** only the as-of views of `CFB_MODEL` (`model/as-of`), plus the previous season's SP+ ratings, which are in the past at every point of a season:
  - `TEAM_SEASON_AS_OF` for `SP_PRIOR_RATING`, `ELO_AS_OF` and `CLASSIFICATION`;
  - `GAME_AS_OF` for the games and their status at the point;
  - `RATING_SP` of season S − 1 for the offense and defense ratings.

  The model reads no result, Elo, line or poll that is not visible at the point, and never the current season's SP+.
- **Parameters:** `CFB_SIM.SCORE_MODEL_PARAMS`, a seeded reference table generated by `make calibrate` (`sim-engine/calibration`), with one row per params version:
  - `CURRENT`, fitted on 2014–2025;
  - `HOLDOUT_2023`, fitted on 2014–2023 for the backtest and the out-of-sample report.

  Its columns are:
  - `HFA` (home field);
  - `B_SP` and `B_ELO` (both features), `B_ELO_ONLY` and `B_SP_ONLY` (one feature), and `ELO_CENTER`;
  - `FBS_DEFAULT_RATING` and `NON_FBS_RATING` (no feature);
  - `SIGMA_1` (the uncertainty one week ahead) and `TAU` (its growth per week);
  - `TOTAL_INTERCEPT`, `TOTAL_SLOPE`, `TOTAL_DEFAULT` and `TOTAL_SIGMA`;
  - `ELO_PROB_SCALE` and `ELO_PROB_HFA` (`sim-engine/win-probability`);
  - the fit's metadata: seasons, fit date, source target, a data fingerprint and `FEATURE_SET`.
- `CFB_SIM.TEAM_RATING_AS_OF`: one row per params version, as-of point and team-season of the point's season, with `RATING` (`DECIMAL(6,2)`, in points against an average FBS team) and `RATING_SOURCE`:

  | `RATING_SOURCE` | When | `RATING` |
  |---|---|---|
  | `SP_ELO` | both an SP+ prior and an Elo | `B_SP × SP_PRIOR_RATING + B_ELO × (ELO_AS_OF − ELO_CENTER)` |
  | `ELO` | an Elo only | `B_ELO_ONLY × (ELO_AS_OF − ELO_CENTER)` |
  | `SP` | an SP+ prior only | `B_SP_ONLY × SP_PRIOR_RATING` |
  | `CLASS_DEFAULT` | neither | `FBS_DEFAULT_RATING` for FBS teams, `NON_FBS_RATING` for every other classification |

  It also carries the previous season's SP+ offense and defense ratings, which feed the totals.
- `CFB_SIM.GAME_SCORE_AS_OF`: one row per params version, as-of point and game of `GAME_AS_OF` at that point whose status is `NEXT` or `REMAINING`, that has teams, involves an FBS team and is a regular-season game (conference championship games included once they are rows). Columns:
  - `HORIZON_WEEKS` = the game's week sequence − the point's week sequence (1 for the next week);
  - `MU` (`DECIMAL(7,2)`, home minus away) = `HFA` (if not neutral) + home `RATING` − away `RATING`;
  - `SIGMA` (`DECIMAL(5,2)`) = √(`SIGMA_1`² + `TAU`² × (`HORIZON_WEEKS` − 1));
  - `TOTAL` (`DECIMAL(5,1)`) = `TOTAL_INTERCEPT` + `TOTAL_SLOPE` × (the sum of both teams' offense and defense ratings) when all four exist, else `TOTAL_DEFAULT`;
  - `HOME_POINTS_PRED` and `AWAY_POINTS_PRED` (`DECIMAL(5,1)`) = (`TOTAL` ± `MU`) / 2;
  - `HOME_WIN_PROB` (`DECIMAL(5,4)`) = ½ × (Φ((`MU` − ½) / `SIGMA`) + Φ((`MU` + ½) / `SIGMA`)). This is the probability that the engine's rounded margin, with an even overtime when it rounds to 0, is a home win, so predictions and simulations agree;
  - `PARAMS_VERSION` and the ratings and sources of both teams.
- `CFB_SIM.NORMAL_CDF(x)`: a SQL function, because Exasol has no error function. It is accurate to 1e-7 for every x.
- Every real value is fixed-scale `DECIMAL` and computed without `DOUBLE` except inside the square root and Φ, whose results are rounded to their scale.
- **Leak-free:** for every point P, the rows of `TEAM_RATING_AS_OF` and `GAME_SCORE_AS_OF` for P do not change when any fact that is not visible at P changes (the invariant of `model/as-of`).
- The examples run over the sim league in the integration-test namespace, with the test parameters loaded into both versions:

  | `HFA` | `B_SP` | `B_ELO` | `B_ELO_ONLY` | `B_SP_ONLY` | `ELO_CENTER` | `FBS_DEFAULT_RATING` | `NON_FBS_RATING` | `SIGMA_1` | `TAU` | `TOTAL_INTERCEPT` | `TOTAL_SLOPE` | `TOTAL_DEFAULT` |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 2.50 | 0.8000 | 0.04000 | 0.05000 | 1.0000 | 1500 | −5.00 | −20.00 | 15.00 | 2.00 | 10.0 | 0.5000 | 52.0 |

  - At 2026 week 9, Ashford (SEC) has an SP+ prior of 20.00, an Elo of 1700, and 2025 offense and defense ratings of 35.0 and 18.0.
  - Brookvale (SEC) has 10.00, 1600, 30.0 and 22.0.
  - Sable (Sun Belt) moved up to FBS in 2026: no SP+ prior, Elo 1550.
  - Lowell (FCS) has neither.
  - Ashford hosts Brookvale in week 12 (not neutral) and Lowell in week 11.

## Scenarios

### Scenario: A team's rating combines its SP+ prior and its current Elo

* *GIVEN* the sim league at 2026 week 9
* *WHEN* `CFB_SIM.TEAM_RATING_AS_OF` is queried for Ashford and Brookvale with params `CURRENT`
* *THEN* Ashford MUST have `RATING` 24.00 and Brookvale 12.00, both with `RATING_SOURCE` `SP_ELO`

### Scenario: Teams without an SP+ prior or an Elo fall back

* *GIVEN* the sim league at 2026 week 9
* *WHEN* `CFB_SIM.TEAM_RATING_AS_OF` is queried for Sable and Lowell
* *THEN* Sable MUST have `RATING` 2.50 with `RATING_SOURCE` `ELO`
* *AND* Lowell MUST have `RATING` −20.00 with `RATING_SOURCE` `CLASS_DEFAULT`

### Scenario: Home field counts only off neutral sites

* *GIVEN* the sim league at 2026 week 9, and its variant in which Ashford against Brookvale is played at a neutral site
* *WHEN* `CFB_SIM.GAME_SCORE_AS_OF` is queried for that game
* *THEN* `MU` MUST be 14.50 at Ashford's home
* *AND* `MU` MUST be 12.00 at the neutral site

### Scenario: The uncertainty grows with the weeks until the game

* *GIVEN* the week-12 game Ashford against Brookvale
* *WHEN* `CFB_SIM.GAME_SCORE_AS_OF` is queried for it at 2026 weeks 7, 9 and 11
* *THEN* `HORIZON_WEEKS` MUST be 5, 3 and 1
* *AND* `SIGMA` MUST be 15.52, 15.26 and 15.00

### Scenario: Predicted points and win probability follow from the margin

* *GIVEN* the week-12 game Ashford against Brookvale at 2026 week 9
* *WHEN* `CFB_SIM.GAME_SCORE_AS_OF` is queried for it
* *THEN* `TOTAL` MUST be 62.5, `HOME_POINTS_PRED` 38.5 and `AWAY_POINTS_PRED` 24.0
* *AND* `HOME_WIN_PROB` MUST be 0.8289

### Scenario: Every game the engine simulates has a prediction

* *GIVEN* the sim league at 2026 week 9
* *WHEN* the games of `CFB_MODEL.GAME_AS_OF` at that point with status `NEXT` or `REMAINING`, with teams, involving an FBS team and in the regular season, are compared with `CFB_SIM.GAME_SCORE_AS_OF`
* *THEN* each of them MUST have exactly one row per params version, with `MU`, `SIGMA`, `TOTAL` and `HOME_WIN_PROB` set and `SIGMA` above 0
* *AND* the week-11 game against the FCS team Lowell MUST be among them

### Scenario: Predictions at a point do not change when later facts change

* *GIVEN* the rows of `TEAM_RATING_AS_OF` and `GAME_SCORE_AS_OF` for 2024 week 9, recorded once
* *AND* every 2024 fact that is not visible at that point is then changed in `CFB_CORE`: results, Elo and lines of weeks 10 and later, polls released after week 9, and the 2024 SP+
* *WHEN* both views are queried again for that point
* *THEN* they MUST return exactly the recorded rows
* *AND* a changed week-9 postgame Elo of Ashford MUST change Ashford's rating at that point, which shows the comparison can detect a change

### Scenario: The params version selects the parameters

* *GIVEN* the test parameters, with `B_SP` changed to 0.6000 in `HOLDOUT_2023` only
* *WHEN* `CFB_SIM.TEAM_RATING_AS_OF` is queried for Ashford at 2026 week 9 for both versions
* *THEN* `CURRENT` MUST give 24.00 and `HOLDOUT_2023` 20.00

### Scenario: Engine-facing values are fixed-scale decimals

* *GIVEN* a deployed simulation schema
* *WHEN* the column types of `TEAM_RATING_AS_OF` and `GAME_SCORE_AS_OF` are read from `EXA_ALL_COLUMNS`
* *THEN* `RATING` MUST be `DECIMAL(6,2)`, `MU` `DECIMAL(7,2)`, `SIGMA` `DECIMAL(5,2)`, `TOTAL` `DECIMAL(5,1)` and `HOME_WIN_PROB` `DECIMAL(5,4)`
* *AND* `CFB_SIM.NORMAL_CDF` MUST agree with the standard normal CDF within 1e-7 at every x from −8 to 8 in steps of 0.01
